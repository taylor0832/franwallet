FranWallet, FranGPT and the existing integration

FranWallet is the product that stores and organizes your location records. Its existing connection is called Fran — Franchise Wallet. You may also see FranGPT used for a franchise-assistant entry point.

Access depends on current availability in your ChatGPT account. If you cannot find FranWallet, contact support for current access options. You can use the public guides and your Wallet without that connection.

Start with an existing Wallet

Create or sign in to FranWallet and organize a location first. When the integration is available to you, use its supported account-connection flow and review the consent screen. It should use only the locations the connected account is permitted to access.

Useful questions about existing records

  • Which of my locations should I review before month-end?
  • What documents are recorded for this location?
  • Show my recorded debt information and its source dates.
  • What accounting snapshots are available, and what period do they cover?

Financial records and source-review tools are read-only. Other capabilities may depend on specific account access or a limited release; confirm current permissions before relying on an editing workflow.

Keep source dates with the answer

Ask for the reporting period, record date and missing information. The integration should distinguish record presence from verification of financial health, accounting close, compliance, valuation or financing eligibility. Use the Wallet to inspect and update the underlying information through supported workflows.
