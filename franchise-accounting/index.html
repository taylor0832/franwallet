What franchise accounting needs to explain

A franchise owner needs to understand sales, operating costs, royalties, advertising contributions, payroll and the result for each reporting period. The supporting records matter as much as the dashboard: which location, which period, which accounting basis and which sources produced the number?

FranWallet organizes location-level records and supported accounting reports. CoverPanda offers bookkeeping services separately. Choose the software, service or combination that fits the work you need done.

Review existing reports in your Wallet

Connect supported QuickBooks accounts to review available profit-and-loss information. Keep the reporting period and last sync date with your questions. Store relevant debt records and business documents beside the location so your accountant has context.

A report snapshot is information for review. Connecting an account does not establish that the books are reconciled, complete or closed.

When you want a bookkeeping team

CoverPanda publishes franchise bookkeeping services covering transaction categorization, reconciliation, royalty and advertising-fund reporting, and financial statements. Engagement terms determine deliverables, timing, systems, fees and responsibilities.

Review CoverPanda bookkeeping scope and current pricing. Professional services and third-party subscriptions are separate from FranWallet software plans.

A useful monthly accounting conversation

  1. Confirm that you are reviewing the right company, location, period and accounting basis.
  2. Check whether bank, card, sales and payroll records are complete for that period.
  3. Ask how royalties and advertising contributions were calculated under your agreement.
  4. Compare revenue and costs with a relevant prior period.
  5. Separate an unusual accounting entry from an actual operating change.
  6. Record one question or next action and who will resolve it.

Multi-location reporting starts with comparable records

Location-level reports are easier to compare when periods, account definitions and treatment of shared costs are consistent. Separate legal entities and intercompany activity may require accounting work beyond viewing stores in one interface. Agree on the reporting structure with your accountant.

Common questions

Does FranWallet replace my accountant?

FranWallet organizes records and helps you review available reports. Bookkeeping, tax preparation and professional accounting advice require their own agreed services.

Can I use FranWallet with my existing accountant?

You can continue using your existing advisors. Confirm the supported account connections and sharing arrangements for your team.

Is bookkeeping included in the free Wallet?

No. Software access and professional services have separate scope and pricing.
