What is a franchise data room?

A franchise data room is an organized set of records shared for a specific purpose, such as a financing discussion or a business sale. It may include financial statements, loan documents, the franchise agreement, lease and other information requested by the recipient.

FranWallet helps keep the location's records together and offers buyer/lender sharing features on eligible plans. Review current plan availability in the Wallet before publishing a room.

Build the package around the request

  • Financial statements: company, period and accounting basis clearly identified.
  • Debt records: statement dates and supporting lender documents.
  • Franchise documents: signed agreement and relevant amendments.
  • Property documents: lease, amendments and other requested records.
  • Insurance and operating records where relevant.
  • A clear list of missing items and unresolved questions.

Ask the buyer, lender or advisor what they need. A general checklist is a starting point, not a complete transaction-specific request list.

Review dates before sharing

A file can be present and still be out of date. Check that the period, location and legal entity match the request. Keep original documents distinguishable from summaries and owner-prepared scenarios. Have the appropriate advisor review consequential financial or legal questions.

Use the sharing controls for the conversation

Choose the files and available access settings deliberately. Eligible FranWallet sharing features include expiring room links and revocation. Confirm the current settings and recipient experience before sharing confidential records.

Revoking access cannot retrieve a copy someone already downloaded. If you need advanced transaction administration, specialized redaction or other controls, evaluate a dedicated virtual data room against those requirements.

Organized records are the start

A room does not verify the underlying records, replace due diligence, establish a business value or secure financing. It helps you make the conversation specific: what is included, the dates it covers and what remains to resolve.

Common questions

Can I use this for an accountant as well as a lender?

Organize the records around the recipient and purpose. Confirm that the current sharing options fit the intended use before sending a link.

Is a complete room proof that I qualify for financing?

No. The lender determines its requirements and eligibility. Document presence is not verification or approval.
