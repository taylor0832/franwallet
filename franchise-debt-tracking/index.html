A debt schedule you can trace to its source

For a franchise owner, a useful debt schedule connects each obligation to the right location and the documents behind it. FranWallet helps keep recorded lender, balance, payment, rate and source-date information together, alongside the location's financial records.

Owner-entered or imported debt records need review against lender information. A bank payment match does not establish the outstanding principal or a payoff amount.

What to keep for each obligation

  • Location and legal borrower.
  • Lender and loan reference, without exposing sensitive account numbers unnecessarily.
  • Recorded balance and the date it represents.
  • Payment amount, rate and maturity information when known.
  • Original agreement, amendments and current statements.
  • Open questions for the lender or accountant.

Keep an unknown field open for follow-up rather than replacing it with a guessed value.

Review a month without losing context

  1. Confirm that each record belongs to the correct location.
  2. Find the statement that supports the recorded balance.
  3. Review the payment and any possible match against connected bank activity.
  4. Check how principal, interest, fees or other changes explain the new statement balance.
  5. Update the recorded amount and source date after review.

Work alongside your accounting system

Your accounting system records liabilities and transactions. FranWallet provides a location-centered place for records and review. Confirm posting and reconciliation with your accountant. A specialist debt-accounting platform may be a better fit when you require complex interest calculations, covenant testing or formal debt disclosures.

Common questions

Does a matched payment verify my loan balance?

No. Confirm the balance and date against the lender statement. Interest, fees, additional borrowing and adjustments can affect the amount owed.

Does FranWallet refinance my loans?

This page describes debt record organization and review. It does not promise refinancing, lender matching or approval.
