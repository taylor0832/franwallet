What does franchise entity setup involve?

Entity setup connects the business you plan to operate with its legal name, ownership, tax identification, banking and operating records. For a franchise owner, the company information also needs to line up with the franchise agreement, lease, lender requirements and service accounts.

FranWallet gives the location a place to keep those details and the next steps together. Creating a Wallet is free to start; it does not create a legal entity or submit a state filing.

Start with the decisions that affect the paperwork

  1. Confirm the proposed owners, operating state and legal entity type with your advisors.
  2. Check how your franchisor, landlord and lender need the entity to appear on their documents.
  3. Keep proposed names separate from a name approved by the state.
  4. Review the filing provider, registered-agent arrangement, fees and ongoing obligations.
  5. After formation, retain the official approval before moving to the applicable EIN, banking and registration tasks.

LLC formation and incorporation are different choices

An LLC and a corporation are state-law entity structures. Incorporation ordinarily refers to creating a corporation; forming an LLC is a different filing. A federal tax election is another decision. FranWallet helps organize the information, while your attorney or tax advisor helps you choose the structure for your circumstances.

Compare incorporation and LLC formation before requesting a filing quote.

Common questions

Does opening a FranWallet form my LLC?

No. A Wallet organizes your company information and setup tasks. State filing requires a separate service process and your authorization.

Can I organize a corporation instead of an LLC?

You can prepare company information for discussion with your advisors and provider. Confirm that the requested entity type, state and service are supported before accepting a quote.

Is the EIN the same as incorporation?

No. An EIN is a federal tax identification number. State formation and federal tax identification are separate steps.
