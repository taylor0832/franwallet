Start with the task in front of you

Franchise services cover different jobs at different stages. A new owner may need entity setup and business records. An operating store may need bookkeeping or payroll support. An owner preparing a lender or buyer conversation needs organized, current documents.

FranWallet is a CoverPanda product for organizing the location and its information. Services are provided under their own scope and commercial terms; a software account alone does not engage a professional or authorize a filing.

Entity setup and incorporation support

Prepare ownership, state and proposed company information, organize formation records and track the steps that follow. Discuss LLC formation or incorporation with an appropriate advisor, then review provider availability, state fees and service terms.

Payroll support and labor records

Keep payroll information organized by location and review the sources behind labor questions. CoverPanda also publishes payroll services. Processing pay, tax filings, registrations and provider integrations depend on the agreed service and supported setup.

Review CoverPanda payroll services. Your service quote should explain provider subscriptions, processing responsibilities and fees.

Debt, documents and professional conversations

Record loan information with source dates and keep original business documents together. Prepare a selected set of information for an accountant, lender or buyer. Storage and sharing help organize a conversation; they do not verify the documents or approve financing.

Review debt organization or prepare a document room.

What to confirm before engaging a provider

  • Who provides the service and who is your contact?
  • What is included, excluded and dependent on your inputs?
  • Which fees are one-time, recurring, state or third-party charges?
  • What must you approve before a filing, payment or other action?
  • What records and access will the provider need?
  • How are documents returned, access revoked and services canceled?

Common questions

Are all franchise services included in FranWallet?

No. The Wallet is software. Professional services, filing support and third-party subscriptions have separate terms and fees.

Does FranWallet sell franchise opportunities?

These pages help people setting up or operating a franchise. They are not a directory of franchises for sale or a promise to match you with a franchise brand.
