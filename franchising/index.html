Who these franchising resources are for

FranWallet serves franchise owner-operators: people preparing a location, running a store or organizing several locations. Use this hub to find the next practical task in your journey. If you want to turn your own brand into a franchise system, you will need a different legal and development process with qualified advisors.

Before choosing or buying a franchise

Understand the Franchise Disclosure Document, the franchise agreement, investment requirements, fees and the experience of existing operators. Separate the franchisor's representations from your own projections, and use independent professional review.

Read the FDD guide and work through unit economics.

After your franchise agreement

Confirm the company structure, legal name, operating state and requirements from your franchisor, lender and landlord. Organize formation, tax identification, banking, insurance and the records needed before opening. The steps depend on your situation; a new Wallet is not a formed company.

Plan entity setup or start your location record.

While you operate

Keep financial reporting, payroll information, loan records, leases and other documents connected to the correct location. Review source dates, follow up on missing information and ask specific questions of the people who help run the business.

Review your accounting options and prepare a weekly cash position.

When you grow, refinance or prepare a sale

Keep each location's records distinct and prepare documents for the actual recipient's request. Review historical statements, debt information, franchise and lease documents with your advisors. A data room makes selected records easier to share; it does not establish a business valuation or lender eligibility.

Prepare buyer or lender documents.
