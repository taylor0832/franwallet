# Check the state rule for the actual transaction.

Canonical article: https://franwallet.com/insights/the-14-registration-states/

## Why the state matters

Federal franchise disclosure rules operate alongside state laws. State requirements can involve registration, notice filings, exemptions, disclosures and other conditions. Which rules apply depends on the parties, transaction and jurisdiction.


## Do not rely on a fixed state count

A page labeled the 14 registration states can hide differences between registration, notice filing and exemptions. Use current state regulator information and qualified franchise counsel to establish the requirements for your situation. Registration should not be treated as a recommendation or guarantee of the investment.


## A verification checklist


- Identify where the offer, sale and operation are relevant.

- Ask counsel which state laws apply.

- Locate the regulator's current official information.

- Check the franchisor's filing, effective dates and any applicable exemption.

- Keep the current FDD and required state materials together.

- Resolve open questions before signing or paying.


## Use the record for the next step

Save the source, date checked and advisor follow-up with your franchise research. FranWallet stores and organizes information; it does not verify registration status, provide a state-law opinion or determine compliance.
