# Understand the sales base behind the fee.

Canonical article: https://franwallet.com/insights/understanding-royalties/

## What is a franchise royalty?

A royalty is an ongoing charge under the franchise arrangement. It may use a sales percentage, fixed amount, minimum or another contractual method. Advertising contributions, required local spending and technology charges may be separate obligations.


## Use the agreement's definition

Find the applicable rate, sales definition, exclusions, minimum, reporting period and payment date. Discounts, refunds, delivery fees or taxes may be treated differently under different agreements. Do not substitute a general definition of gross sales for the contract.


## A worked calculation

Illustrative agreement: a 6% royalty on a $100,000 contractual sales base produces $6,000. A separate 2% advertising contribution on that same base adds $2,000. The combined $8,000 is not a claim about a typical franchise or your actual agreement.


## Reconcile the period


- Confirm the applicable agreement and fee terms.

- Match the sales report to the charge period.

- Check adjustments, exclusions and any minimum.

- Compare the calculation with the statement and actual draw.

- Raise unexplained differences with supporting records.


## Keep fees visible in the business picture

Discuss accounting treatment with your bookkeeper and include dated cash obligations in your planning. FranWallet helps organize records and reports. It does not interpret your agreement or resolve a fee dispute.
