# Read the FDD as a set of questions to investigate.

Canonical article: https://franwallet.com/insights/what-is-an-fdd/

## What is a Franchise Disclosure Document?

The FDD is the franchisor's disclosure document for a prospective franchise buyer. It contains information about the system, investment, fees, obligations and associated agreements. Read it with the franchise agreement and independent advice rather than treating it as a sales brochure.


## Group the questions before reading


- Who is the franchisor and what history should I investigate?

- What is the estimated investment, and which ongoing fees apply?

- What obligations, territory and supplier restrictions would I accept?

- What financial information is actually represented, and which outlets does it cover?

- What do openings, closures, transfers and franchisee conversations reveal?

- What agreements would I sign, and how do renewal, transfer and termination work?


## Keep timing and professional review in view

The FTC's Franchise Rule includes a disclosure waiting period before certain agreements or payments to the franchisor or its affiliate. Review the current rule, transaction circumstances and any state requirements with counsel. Do not assume that receiving an FDD means the government has approved the business opportunity.


## Turn the reading into follow-up

Keep the document version and date, record the question and relevant item, and ask for clarification or supporting material. Compare projections with the disclosed basis and speak with operators. Organize documents in FranWallet while your attorney and accountant address the legal and financial questions.
