Prepare a useful monthly bookkeeping review.

A franchise bookkeeping checklist for source records, reconciliation questions, royalties, payroll, financial reports and location-specific follow-up.

Start with the reporting period

Confirm the company, location, month and accounting basis. Note whether the month is still open, has been reviewed or has been closed by the responsible accountant. Downloading a report is not the same as completing the accounting work.

Gather the source records

  • Bank and card statements, including missing pages and adjustments.
  • Sales and payment-processor reports for the same period.
  • Payroll reports and related liabilities.
  • Royalty and advertising-fund statements.
  • Invoices, receipts, lease payments and unusual purchases.
  • Loan statements and information about new borrowing.

Ask what has been reconciled

Ask your bookkeeper whether bank and card balances have been reconciled, whether sales deposits and processor deductions have been explained, and which transactions still need owner information. Check whether late adjustments will change the statements.

Review the franchise-specific lines

Compare royalty and advertising charges with the contractual sales definition and relevant period. Check payroll timing, shared costs and the treatment of owner items with your accountant. Use consistent location definitions before comparing stores.

Leave with a report and a clear question

Review the P&L and balance sheet with their period and basis. Ask about the largest change or unresolved item and record who will follow up. FranWallet helps organize records and available reports; CoverPanda bookkeeping is a separate engagement with agreed deliverables and fees.
