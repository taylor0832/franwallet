Before signing documents in a company name

Identify the intended legal party with your advisors. Confirm who signs the franchise agreement and lease, how the company name should appear, and whether landlord, lender or franchisor consent is needed for a later change. A proposed name is not a state-approved company.

Prepare the formation information

  • Proposed entity type, filing state and legal name.
  • Owners, ownership interests and authorized signers.
  • Registered-agent and business-address arrangements.
  • Franchisor, lease and lender naming requirements.
  • Provider scope, state fees, renewal costs and required approvals.

Keep sensitive owner identity information out of a general public checklist or email attachment.

After state formation

Keep the state approval, effective date and governing documents. If creating a corporation or LLC, the IRS directs you to form it with the state before applying for the EIN. An EIN can be requested directly from the IRS without a fee; a provider may charge separately for assistance.

Prepare for business banking

Ask your bank for its actual document list. It may need formation evidence, tax identification, ownership and signer information, governing documents and information about the business. Check that names are consistent. Do not treat a generic checklist as the bank's account-opening approval.

Connect setup with opening operations

Arrange the appropriate insurance, registrations, books and payroll before the relevant operating or hiring activity. Identify deadlines from official notices and your advisors. Formation approval alone does not establish that all licenses, employer registrations or opening requirements are complete.
